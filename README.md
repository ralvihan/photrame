<h1 align="center">Photrame</h1>

<p align="center"><b>Capture the moment.</b></p>

Free online photobooth. No login, no uploads.

Take photos with your camera or upload your own, pick a frame, and download your photo strip. Right in your browser.

🔗 **Try it:** [photrame.vercel.app](https://photrame.vercel.app)

## How it works

1. **Snap**: open your camera and strike a pose, or upload your photos.
2. **Pick**: choose a layout and a frame, then adjust your shots.
3. **Keep**: download your photo strip as PNG.

## Features

- Camera photobooth with timer and multiple shots
- Upload your own photos
- Photo strip layouts
- Frame library: Classic (Black, White, Dark Gray, Navy) and Originals (custom frames), with separate color options
- Filters, brightness, and contrast
- Adjust each photo: drag to move, zoom to fit
- Hand-drawn signature (3 thicknesses) on the Photrame (1×1) frame
- Download as PNG, or save your raw shots

## Privacy

Your photos stay on your device. Photrame doesn't ask you to sign in, and it doesn't upload your photos anywhere.
